FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions about MakerQuest? Here are answers to the most common questions from parents and teachers.

Do I need to buy expensive robotics kits, electronics, or specialized tools?

No, absolutely not! The core mission of MakerQuest is 100% zero-waste scrap engineering. All you need are clean cardboard boxes (like Amazon, Flipkart, or cereal cartons), drinking straws, wooden skewers, rubber bands, household scissors, and glue. Everything is built from scratch.

What age group is MakerQuest suitable for?

MakerQuest is designed for Indian students from Class 3 to Class 10 (ages 7 to 16). Younger inventors (ages 7–9) enjoy building with parental or elder sibling guidance, while older cadets (ages 10–16) work independently through the interactive 3D CAD blueprints, kinematic formulas, and checkpoint challenges.

Is this a monthly subscription or a one-time purchase?

MakerQuest is a single one-time payment of ₹199. You receive permanent lifetime access to all 12 chapters, all 17+ interactive 3D blueprints, CAD simulator tools, and all future updates with zero recurring fees.

How is this different from watching free YouTube videos?

YouTube videos are passive, unstructured, and often leave students frustrated with missing dimensions or unverified mechanics. MakerQuest provides interactive 3D CAD models you can rotate and zoom, step-by-step progress tracking, real physics formulas, error-prevention cards, and verifiable STEM certifications.

What if my child doesn't enjoy it? Do you offer a guarantee?

We offer an unconditional 30-day 100% money-back guarantee. If your student doesn't build their first working machine or isn't completely captivated, simply let us know and we will issue an immediate full refund of ₹199 to your UPI ID — no questions asked.

Can Indian schools, Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL), or homeschoolers use MakerQuest?

Yes! MakerQuest is fully aligned with the Indian National Education Policy (NEP 2020) experiential learning mandate and CBSE/ICSE practical physics standards. It is perfect for Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL) and school science clubs, including educator lesson summaries and zero-cost material guides.
